In this assignment, I utilized LangChain's Routing and Runnable Chains. First, a router chain was used to classify user queries. For questions that met the criteria, I customized prompts for each extraction task and designed functions to loop through each invoice image. This approach was taken to circumvent the LLM API's token limits, ensuring that only a single image was sent to the LLM for processing at a time, and the results from each image were then aggregated to obtain the final analysis
